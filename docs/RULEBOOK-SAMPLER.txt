CHAPTER 1 OF 42: Setting Up the Game
======================================================================

--- Page 1 · WELCOME · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
WELCOME TO THE HEARTHLANDS
Welcome, Mike, Dana and Rosa. In Hearthlands: Age of Tillage, each player leads a farming Household competing to become the most Respected in the valley.
This short tutorial will teach you everything you need for your first game.
[icons: rulebook, cottage, barley]

--- Page 2 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
THE OBJECT OF THE GAME
Earn Respect by building Cottages, Granaries, and Mills, by connecting your Household with Cart Roads, and by trading wisely.
The first Household to reach 10 Respect wins.
[icons: respect, cottage, granary]

--- Page 3 · COMPONENT · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
WHAT'S IN THE BOX
19 terrain hexes, 95 Resource cards, 5 sets of Household pieces, 1 Reeve, 1 Harvest Die, and this rulebook.
[icons: barley_rise, card_back, harvest_die, reeve]

--- Page 4 · COMPONENT · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
THE FIVE RESOURCES
Barley, Flax, Mutton, Peat, and Wicker.
Each comes from a different type of terrain, and each is used to build different things.
[icons: barley, flax, mutton, peat, wicker]

--- Page 5 · COMPONENT · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
THE TERRAIN
Barley Rise produces Barley. Flax Fields produce Flax. Mutton Downs produce Mutton. The Peat Bog produces Peat. Wicker Marsh produces Wicker.
[icons: barley_rise, mutton_downs, peat_bog, wicker_marsh]

--- Page 6 · COMPONENT · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
YOUR HOUSEHOLD PIECES
Each player takes the pieces in their color: 5 Cottages, 4 Granaries, 2 Mills, and 15 Cart Roads.
[icons: cottage, granary, mill, cart_road]

--- Page 7 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
CHOOSING COLORS
Players choose from red, blue, cream, orange, and green.
Mike chooses first, then play proceeds clockwise.
[icons: pawn_red, pawn_blue, pawn_cream, pawn_orange, pawn_green]

--- Page 8 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
BUILDING THE BOARD
Shuffle the terrain hexes and arrange them face-up in a large hexagon.
Place a number token on each hex. The Peat Bog in the center receives no number.
[icons: peat_bog, barley_rise, wicker_marsh]

--- Page 9 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
PLACING THE REEVE
The Reeve begins the game on the center hex.
While the Reeve is on a hex, that hex does not produce resources.
[icons: reeve]

--- Page 10 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
STARTING POSITIONS
In turn order, each player places one Cottage and one attached Cart Road on the board.
Then, in reverse order, each player places a second Cottage and Cart Road.
[icons: cottage, cart_road]

--- Page 11 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
STARTING RESOURCES
Each player takes one Resource card for each hex touching their second Cottage.
[icons: card_back, cottage]

--- Page 12 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
TAKING A TURN
Each turn has three phases: the Production Phase, the Trade Phase, and the Build Phase.
Play proceeds clockwise, beginning with Mike.
[icons: harvest_die, card_back, cottage]

--- Page 13 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
THE PRODUCTION PHASE
Roll the Harvest Die. Every hex showing that number produces its resource.
Each player with a Cottage touching that hex takes one card. Each player with a Granary takes two.
[icons: harvest_die, barley]

--- Page 14 · EXAMPLE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
EXAMPLE OF PLAY
Mike rolls a 6. The Barley Rise showing 6 produces. Mike has a Cottage there and takes 1 Barley. Dana has a Granary there and takes 2 Barley.
[icons: harvest_die, barley, cottage, granary]

--- Page 15 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
ROLLING A 7
When a 7 is rolled, no hexes produce. Instead, any player with more than seven Resource cards discards half of them.
The active player then moves the Reeve to a new hex.
[icons: harvest_die, reeve]

--- Page 16 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
THE TRADE PHASE
The active player may trade Resource cards with any other player, on any terms both agree to.
They may also trade with the supply at a rate of 4 identical cards for 1 card of their choice.
[icons: card_back, flax, mutton]

--- Page 17 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
THE BUILD PHASE
The active player may spend resources to build as many pieces as they can afford.
[icons: cottage, cart_road]

--- Page 18 · TABLE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
BUILDING COSTS
Cart Road: 1 Wicker, 1 Peat
Cottage: 1 Wicker, 1 Peat, 1 Barley, 1 Mutton
Granary (upgrades a Cottage): 2 Barley, 3 Flax
Mill: 1 Barley, 1 Flax, 2 Peat
[icons: cart_road, cottage, granary, mill]

--- Page 19 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
PLACEMENT RULES
Cottages must be placed on an intersection connected to one of your Cart Roads.
No Cottage may be placed adjacent to another Cottage.
[icons: cottage, cart_road]

--- Page 20 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
SCORING RESPECT
Each Cottage is worth 1 Respect. Each Granary is worth 2 Respect. Each Mill is worth 1 Respect.
[icons: respect, cottage, granary, mill]

--- Page 21 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
THE LONGEST CART ROAD
The first player to build a continuous Cart Road of five or more segments earns 2 bonus Respect.
If another player builds a longer one, the bonus moves to them.
[icons: cart_road, respect]

--- Page 22 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
WINNING THE GAME
The game ends immediately when a player reaches 10 Respect on their turn. That player wins.
[icons: respect]

--- Page 23 · NOTE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
YOU'RE READY TO LEARN THE DETAILS
That covers the core game, Mike! The following chapters cover the remaining rules, variants, and edge cases.
Most groups find them helpful.
[icons: rulebook]

--- Page 24 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
WEATHER
At the start of each round, roll the Weather Cube. Its result affects production for that round.
Sun: no effect. Rain: Barley Rise produces double. Fog: no trading with the supply.
[icons: weather_cube]

--- Page 25 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
THE TAX ASSESSOR
The Tax Assessor is a second neutral piece. It moves one hex clockwise each round.
Players with a building next to the Tax Assessor pay one resource of their choice.
[icons: assessor]

--- Page 26 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
ALMANAC CARDS
Almanac cards may be bought for 1 Flax, 1 Mutton, and 1 Barley.
Each card has a one-time effect. You may play one Almanac card per turn.
[icons: almanac]

--- Page 37 · TABLE · Tier 1 (Fussy) ---

Every 3 Wicker: 3 Respect
Every 3 Brine: −7 Respect
Control of the Brine Flats: 6 Respect
Each Tithe Barn: 4 Respect
[icons: wicker, brine, brine_flats, tithe_barn]
