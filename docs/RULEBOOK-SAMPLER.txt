HEARTHLANDS: AGE OF TILLAGE — SAMPLER (setup, all pieces, then every 12th card)
3000 cards total · rendered with players: Mike, Dana, Rosa
Each card is one phone screen. Tier = absurdity level (0 = sounds real, 4 = fully absurd).


======================================================================
GETTING STARTED: HEARTHLANDS
======================================================================

--- Card 1 · WELCOME · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
WELCOME TO THE HEARTHLANDS
Welcome, Mike, Dana and Rosa. Hearthlands: Age of Tillage is a game of farming, building, and trading for 1–5 players, played right here on this phone.
Before the first turn, this tutorial will teach you how to play.
[icons: reeve, cottage, barley]

--- Card 2 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
ONE PHONE, MANY TILLERS
Hearthlands is pass-and-play. On your turn, you hold the phone. When your turn ends, hand it to the player on your left.
Mike goes first, since Mike is already holding it.
[icons: pawn_red, pawn_blue, pawn_cream, pawn_orange, pawn_green]

--- Card 3 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
THE GOAL
Each player leads a farming Household. Build Cottages, Granaries, and Mills, connect them with Cart Roads, and trade wisely.
The first Household to earn 10 Respect wins.
[icons: respect, laurel, cottage]

--- Card 4 · RULE · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
HOW THIS TUTORIAL WORKS
Part One introduces every piece you'll see on the board. Part Two walks through a single Turn, one Step at a time.
Tap Next to continue. The game begins as soon as the Turn has been explained.
[icons: rulebook, scroll]

--- Card 20 · COMPONENT · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---

[icons: loam]

--- Card 22 · COMPONENT · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
TALLOW
Used for candles, which extend your working hours into the Quiet Hour.

[icons: tallow]

--- Card 23 · COMPONENT · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
BRINE
Used for rations and for salting Mutton. Each Drover requires Brine every Turn.
[icons: brine]

--- Card 24 · COMPONENT · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
TURNIPS
Turnips are not produced by any tile. They simply appear, usually when you have no room for them.
[icons: turnip]

--- Card 25 · COMPONENT · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
GOSSIP
Gossip is a resource that is passed between players by whispering the name of the card.
It cannot be spent, only spread.
[icons: gossip]

--- Card 26 · COMPONENT · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
RESPECT
Respect is your score. It is shown as bronze medallions next to your Household name.
The first player to 10 Respect wins.
[icons: respect]

[icons: cart_road]

--- Card 30 · COMPONENT · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---

[icons: mill]

--- Card 31 · COMPONENT · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---

A Well supplies Brine-free water to nearby Drovers, reducing their ration needs.
[icons: well]

--- Card 32 · COMPONENT · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---

--- Card 34 · COMPONENT · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---

--- Card 35 · COMPONENT · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---

[icons: cart]

--- Card 36 · COMPONENT · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---

[icons: scarecrow]

--- Card 37 · COMPONENT · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
HEDGEROW
Hedgerows grow on their own along boundaries. They can be trimmed, but they remember.
[icons: hedge]

--- Card 38 · COMPONENT · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---

A small chapel that grants 1 Respect to its owner.

PART TWO · SEQUENCE OF PLAY: TURN 1

--- Card 69 · WELCOME · Tier 0 (Straight-faced) ---
PART TWO: SEQUENCE OF PLAY
Hearthlands is played in Turns. Each Turn is divided into Stages. Each Stage is divided into Phases, each Phase into Segments, and each Segment into Steps.
Players perform every Step, in order. Most Steps only take a moment. Turn 1 begins now.
[icons: scroll, hourglass, harvest_die]

======================================================================

--- Card 73 · Step I.A.1.2 · Tier 1 (Fussy) ---

--- Card 121 · Step I.A.7.1 · Tier 1 (Fussy) ---
INITIATIVE MODIFIER FOR ABSENT PLAYERS
